Top 3 Fastest Creatures of Land and Air

Top 3 Fastest Creatures on Land

  1. Cheetah

    Four characteristics of Cheetah

    • The Cheetah (Acinonyx jubatus) is the fastest creature on land. It can accelerate from 0 to 97km/h (60 mph) under 3 seconds.
    • Cheetahs top speed averages between 96 - 112km/h (60 - 70mph)
    • Cheetahs can not roar. They make purr sound when inhaling and exhaling.
    • The average lifespan of Cheetahs is 8 to 10 years in their natural habitat.

  2. Pronghorn

    Four characteristics of Pronghorn

    • The Pronghorn (Antilocapra americana) is the second fastest creature on land. It can accelerate from 0 to 60 mph in about 3 seconds.
    • Pronghorn top speed averages between 65 - 88km/h (40 - 55mph).
    • Under favorable conditions they can reach a top speed of 97km/h (60mph). They also have high endurance.
    • The average lifespan of a Pronghorn is 7 to 10 years.

  3. Springbok

    Four characteristics of Springbok

    • The Springbok (Antidorcas marsupialis) is the third fastest creature on land. It can accelerate from 0 to 55mph in about 3 seconds.
    • Springbok top speed averages between 80 - 88km/h (50 - 55mph) and can reach up to 88 - 90 km/h (55 - 56mph)
    • They are also known for their unique jumping style known as pronking.
    • The average lifespan of a Springbok is 7 to 10 years.

Top 3 Fastest Creature in Air

  1. Peregrine Falcon

    Four characteristics of Peregrine falcon

    • The Peregrine Falcon (Falco peregrinus) is the fastest creature in air.
    • The maximum top speed of Peregrine Falcon is 389km/h (242mph).
    • It achieves its highest velocity during vertical flight when hunting not in horizontal flight.
    • The average lifespan of Peregrine Falcon in the wild is roughly about 6 to 13 years. The oldest recorded have lived between 19-22 years.

  2. Golden Eagle

    Four characteristics of Golden Eagle

    • The Golden Eagle (Aquila chrysaetos) is the second fastest creature in air.
    • The maximum top speed of Golden Eagle is 322km/h (200mph).
    • Their eyes are roughly the same size as humans, but have 8 times sharper vision.
    • The lifespan of Golden Eagle in wild is between 15 to 30 years, with an average lifespan of about 23 years.

  3. Saker Falcon

    Four characteristics of Saker Falcon

    • The Saker Falcon (Falco cherrug) is the third fastest creature in air.
    • The maximum top speed of Saker Falcon is 322km/h (200mph) same as Golden Eagle but they achieve this speed thanks to their highly aerodynamic body shape.
    • They possess exceptional aerial agility and physical strength to hunt large birds.
    • The average lifespan of Saker Falcon in wild is roughly about 5 to 7 years.
